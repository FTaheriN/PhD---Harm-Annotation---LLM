**Content Leakage:** Intentional leaking of personal information, media, imagery, or other personally identifiable information (PII). This category also includes doxing and the solicitation of personal information for doxing.

**Overloading:** Attempting to put a target in a state where they are flooded with notifications, messages, calls, or otherwise that they cannot manage.

**Public Opinion Manipulation:** Spreading narratives with the direct intent of manipulating public perception.

**Reporting:** Deceiving an online reporting system or institutional authority. Includes swatting and mass account reporting for platform policy violations that may not actually have occurred.

**Reputational Harm:** Publicly or privately harassing an individual's family, employer, or others with the intent of damaging their reputation.

**Physical Harm:** Harm to the target's person or possessions, including carrying out, planning, or encouraging such harm.

**Surveillance:** Following or monitoring an individual and reporting the results online with the intent of exposing otherwise private behaviour.

**Toxic Content:** Directly contacting the target to deliver harassment, including hate speech, unwanted explicit content, or otherwise inflammatory remarks that are unwanted by the target.
